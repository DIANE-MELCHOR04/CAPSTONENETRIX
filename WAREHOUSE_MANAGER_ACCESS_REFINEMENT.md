# Warehouse Manager Access Refinement

The Warehouse Manager keeps **Manage Master Data** and retains oversight and approval responsibilities, but no longer receives unrelated operational or administrative functions.

## Removed from Warehouse Manager

- Creating or bulk-importing new material records
- Viewing Audit Logs
- Creating, dispatching, or receiving transfers
- Creating or bulk-importing stock counts
- Acknowledging, dismissing, converting, or linking AI recommendations
- Workflow Control actions such as void requests, supplier returns, and write-offs

## Retained for Warehouse Manager

- Manage Master Data
- Review and approve submitted material records
- View inventory, movements, dashboards, reports, forecasts, notifications, and calendars
- Approve or reject material requests
- View and approve or reject transfers
- View and approve stock counts
- Approve damage reports and inventory adjustments
- Export authorized operational reports

Transfer execution and stock-count entry remain with Warehouse Personnel. AI recommendation actions remain with Procurement. Workflow Control is restricted to the Administrator.
